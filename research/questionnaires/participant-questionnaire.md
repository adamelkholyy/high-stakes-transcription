# Participant questionnaire

Static archive of the short end questionnaire used for the participant study.
This file is not connected to the prototype and does not collect responses.

All rating questions are required. The final open-text comment is optional.

## Your task experience

These brief questions are about how the tasks felt, not about evaluating the
overall study design.

1. **How confident are you in the transcription corrections you made?** (`confidence_corrections`)
   - Scale: 1 = Not at all confident; 5 = Very confident
2. **How confident are you that your answers to the case questions were supported by the recordings?** (`confidence_answers`)
   - Scale: 1 = Not at all confident; 5 = Very confident
3. **The time available allowed me to make useful progress on each task.** (`time_sufficient`)
   - Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree
4. **How mentally demanding did the tasks feel?** (`mental_demand`)
   - Scale: 1 = Not demanding; 5 = Very demanding

## Interface experience

1. **How useful were the highlights for deciding where to inspect or listen again?** (`highlight_help`)
   - Scale: 1 = Not useful; 5 = Very useful; or Not sure
2. **How useful were the AI tools for locating evidence and answering the case questions?** (`tools_help`)
   - Scale: 1 = Not useful; 5 = Very useful; or Not sure
3. **The interface was easy to understand and use during the tasks.** (`ease_of_use`)
   - Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree
4. **Overall, the interface helped me review the recordings effectively.** (`overall_support`)
   - Scale: 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree

## Optional comment

**What most helped or hindered you while completing the tasks?** (`task_comment`, optional, open text)
